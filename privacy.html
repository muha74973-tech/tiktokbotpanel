Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

TikTokBotPanel is a web application designed to help users view available statistics about their own TikTok videos using official TikTok APIs.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted by the user and TikTok, the application may access basic profile information and available video information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the application's features, display available video statistics, and maintain the security and operation of the service.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable law, or when authorized by the user.

5. Data Security

We will take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service or meet legal obligations. Users may contact the service operator to request deletion of their information.
